About Us

Iraq Business Manager is a developing AI-assisted business management platform intended to support small businesses, particularly businesses operating in Iraq. The project brings together business information, workflow automation, product research, and customer management in one organized system.

The platform is being developed to help business owners make informed decisions while maintaining control over important commercial activities.

Our Commitment

We aim to build practical, transparent, and responsible business tools. AI-generated analysis can support decisions, but it does not guarantee commercial results or replace the business owner's judgment and approval.

Our Services

The platform is being developed around the following business functions. Individual capabilities depend on their implementation, configuration, and the permissions granted by connected services.

📦

Product and Supplier Research

Organize product information, review supplier details, compare available prices, and assess potential resale opportunities.

🛍️

Order Management

Maintain structured records for products, customers, suppliers, orders, and related business activities.

💬

Customer Communication

Support the organization of customer inquiries, customer information, and potential sales conversations through configured workflows.

📱

Social Media Management

Develop authorized workflows for connected social media accounts, subject to the functionality and permissions available through each platform's APIs.

📊

Business Insights

Organize business records and support the review of costs, sales, potential profit, and commercial opportunities using available information.

⚙️

Workflow Automation

Use configured automation workflows to reduce repetitive tasks and support consistent business recordkeeping and operations.
