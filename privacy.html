Iraq Business Manager

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Iraq Business Manager is a developing business workflow automation project designed to support product research, customer and order management, business information, and authorized social media integrations. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and deleted when the service's features are used.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features implemented and used, the information provided by the operator, and the permissions granted, the service may process the following categories of information:

  • Account identifiers and profile information made available by a connected platform.
  • Public profile, video, post, comment, and engagement information accessible through authorized platform APIs.
  • Content, comments, and messages only when the relevant platform and granted permissions make them available.
  • Product details, supplier information, customer inquiries, and order records entered into or processed by the service.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain configured workflows.
  • Access tokens, refresh tokens, and related authorization information required to maintain a connected account, where the integration supports them.

The information actually processed depends on the connected account, enabled features, granted permissions, and the capabilities of each third-party platform. We do not claim access to information that has not been authorized or made available to the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used for the following purposes, where applicable:

  • Maintaining connections to social media accounts authorized by the account owner.
  • Supporting permitted content upload, publishing, and social media management workflows as those features become available.
  • Organizing available comments and customer inquiries and assisting with customer communication where supported.
  • Maintaining product, supplier, customer, and order records.
  • Analyzing available business or public content information to support research and decision-making.
  • Maintaining service reliability, protecting accounts, preventing misuse, and troubleshooting errors.
  • Meeting applicable legal obligations.

3. TikTok and Other Platform Information

When an operator connects a TikTok account, the service may access information or perform actions only to the extent permitted by the granted permissions, TikTok's API capabilities, and applicable TikTok policies. The same principle applies to Facebook, Instagram, and other connected platforms.

Access to account information, public content, comments, messages, analytics, and publishing features depends on the relevant platform's APIs, approvals, permissions, and implementation status. Planned features may not yet be available.

Each third-party platform processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. Users should review the policies of the platforms they connect.

4. Authentication Tokens

For supported social media integrations, the service may store access tokens and refresh tokens in its configured n8n data storage, including the social_accounts data table, to maintain authorized account connections and renew access when supported.

These tokens can grant access within the scope of the associated authorization. They should be treated as confidential credentials, protected from unauthorized access, and used only for the purposes permitted by the relevant platform and the account owner's authorization.

5. Automated Processing and Artificial Intelligence

Certain configured features may use automation or artificial intelligence to organize information, summarize available content, support business research, or assist with preparing customer responses. Automated outputs may be inaccurate and may require human review.

Information sent to third-party AI, hosting, or automation providers may be processed under those providers' own terms, privacy policies, and data-handling practices. Only providers actually used by the service should be treated as recipients of information.

6. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or shared with connected platforms when necessary to operate a feature authorized by the account owner.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or legal rights.

Information should not be shared with third parties for unrelated purposes without appropriate authorization or another lawful basis.

7. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in the databases, data tables, and other systems configured by the service operator and its providers. This may include n8n data storage used for business records and authorized social account connection information.

Appropriate technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Access to credentials and authentication tokens should be restricted to authorized systems and operators that require them.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide configured services, maintain legitimate business records, meet applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, and protect security.

When information is no longer required, the service operator should delete it or arrange for its deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

Disconnecting a social media account may stop future access through that integration, but it may not automatically delete information already stored in other systems. A separate deletion request may be necessary.

9. User Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and platform capabilities, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with their use of the service.

Users may revoke permissions or disconnect an authorized integration through the relevant platform where supported. Revoking access may prevent connected features from continuing to operate.

Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable law, verification where necessary, and legitimate data retention requirements.

10. Children's Privacy

Iraq Business Manager is intended for business use and is not directed toward children. The service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

11. Third-Party Services and External Links

The service may rely on third-party platforms and providers for authorized integrations, hosting, automation, or AI functionality. Those providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Iraq Business Manager does not control the independent privacy practices, availability, or security policies of third-party services.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the service, its integrations, or applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
