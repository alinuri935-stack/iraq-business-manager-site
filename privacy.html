Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Iraq Business Manager is a business workflow automation service designed to help business operators manage authorized social media accounts, content, customer inquiries, products, orders, and business information. This policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, shared, and deleted when the service is used.

1. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features used, the information provided by the operator, and the permissions granted, Iraq Business Manager may process the following categories of information:

  • Account identifiers and profile information made available by a connected platform.
  • Public profile, video, post, comment, and engagement information accessible through authorized platform APIs.
  • Content, comments, and messages when the relevant platform and approved permissions make them available.
  • Product details, supplier information, customer inquiries, order records, and other business information entered into or processed by the service.
  • Technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the service.
  • Authentication tokens and authorization information required to maintain a connection to a platform, where supported.

The information actually processed depends on the connected account, enabled features, granted permissions, and the capabilities of each third-party platform. We do not claim access to information that has not been authorized or made available to the service.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Connect and manage social media accounts authorized by the account owner.
  • Support permitted content upload, publishing, and social media management workflows.
  • Review available comments and customer inquiries and assist with customer communication when supported by the platform and enabled permissions.
  • Organize product, supplier, customer, and order information.
  • Analyze available public content and engagement information to support business research and decision-making when the required access is available.
  • Maintain service reliability, protect accounts, prevent misuse, and troubleshoot errors.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. TikTok and Other Platform Information

When an operator connects a TikTok account, the service may access information and perform actions only as permitted by the permissions granted, TikTok's API capabilities, and applicable TikTok policies. The same principle applies to Facebook, Instagram, and other connected platforms.

The availability of account information, public content, comments, messages, analytics, and publishing features depends on the relevant platform's APIs, approvals, and access permissions. The service does not guarantee that every platform feature will be available.

Each platform processes information under its own privacy policy and terms. Users should review the policies of the platforms they connect.

4. Automated Processing and AI

Certain features may use automated processing or artificial intelligence to organize information, summarize available content, assist with business workflows, or help prepare customer responses. Automated outputs may be inaccurate and may require human review.

The service should process information only to the extent necessary for its enabled functions and authorized integrations. Information sent to third-party AI or automation providers may be subject to those providers' own terms, privacy policies, and data-handling practices.

5. Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed or shared when necessary to provide an authorized feature, including with connected social media platforms, hosting providers, automation services, AI providers, or other service providers used by the operator.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law or when reasonably necessary to protect the service, its users, or legal rights.

The service should not share information with third parties for unrelated purposes without appropriate authorization or another lawful basis.

6. Data Storage and Security

Information may be stored in the databases, data tables, or other systems configured by the service operator and its authorized providers. The exact storage location and retention period depend on the systems and configuration used.

Reasonable technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, loss, misuse, or disclosure. Access to credentials and authentication tokens should be restricted to authorized systems and operators that require them.

No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain legitimate business records, meet applicable legal requirements, resolve disputes, and protect security.

When information is no longer required, the service operator should delete it or arrange for its deletion, subject to applicable legal obligations and legitimate retention requirements.

Disconnecting a social media account may stop future access through that integration, but it may not automatically delete information already stored in other systems. A separate deletion request may be necessary.

8. User Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law and platform capabilities, users may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with their use of the service.

Users may also revoke permissions or disconnect an authorized integration through the relevant platform where supported. Revoking access may prevent connected features from continuing to operate.

Requests will be reviewed and handled subject to applicable law, verification of the request where necessary, and legitimate data retention requirements.

9. Children's Privacy

Iraq Business Manager is intended for business use and is not directed toward children. The service is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

10. Third-Party Services and External Links

The service may rely on third-party platforms and providers to deliver authorized integrations, hosting, automation, or AI functionality. These providers may process information according to their own terms and privacy policies.

Iraq Business Manager does not control the independent privacy practices, availability, or security policies of third-party services.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated when the service, its integrations, or applicable legal requirements change. Updates will be published on this page with a revised date.
